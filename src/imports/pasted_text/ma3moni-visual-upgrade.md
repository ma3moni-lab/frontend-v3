MA3MONI — VISUAL EXPERIENCE UPGRADE PROMPT

ROLE

Act as a senior product designer, visual designer and design-system specialist working on an existing premium matchmaking platform called Ma3moni.

Your task is to upgrade the visual appearance and design quality of the existing Ma3moni platform without redesigning the product.

This is a visual transformation, not a feature redesign.

The existing Ma3moni pages, features, navigation, information architecture, user journeys, interactions and process flows are already defined and must remain intact.

Your objective is to make the existing product feel:

Cinematic + Editorial + Contemporary Romance + Quiet Premium

with a subtle influence of a modern private members’ club where appropriate.

The finished product should feel like it was designed by an experienced human design team—not generated from a generic AI website template.

⸻

1. NON-NEGOTIABLE: DO NOT REDESIGN THE PRODUCT

Preserve the existing:

* Pages
* Navigation
* Menus
* User journeys
* Information architecture
* Features
* Functional requirements
* Buttons and their purposes
* Forms
* Profile functionality
* Matchmaking functionality
* Messaging functionality
* Subscription functionality
* Compatibility functionality
* Search/filter functionality
* Authentication flow
* Account/profile management
* Existing process flows
* Existing content hierarchy

Do not introduce new features simply because they would look visually attractive.

Do not remove existing features.

Do not move functionality into a different process.

Do not change how users accomplish tasks.

Do not invent additional screens.

Do not convert the platform into a different type of dating product.

If an existing component works functionally, keep it and visually refine it.

The goal is:

Same Ma3moni. Same functionality. Same journey. Significantly better visual experience.

Think of this as taking an existing product designed by a competent team and giving it the visual refinement of a world-class design studio.

⸻

2. CORE CREATIVE DIRECTION

Use this visual formula:

70% — CINEMATIC EDITORIAL

Use:

* Editorial composition
* Strong photography
* Sophisticated typography
* Natural visual rhythm
* Carefully controlled asymmetry
* Large image moments
* Varied image scale
* Intentional whitespace
* Beautiful visual hierarchy
* Magazine-inspired composition

Avoid making every section look like a conventional SaaS dashboard.

⸻

20% — CONTEMPORARY ROMANCE

The platform should feel:

* Warm
* Human
* Intimate
* Sophisticated
* Emotionally intelligent
* Approachable
* Authentic

Romance should be expressed through photography, typography, spacing, transitions and subtle details—not through excessive hearts, pink gradients or decorative romantic graphics.

⸻

10% — MODERN PRIVATE CLUB

Use this selectively for:

* Premium/subscription areas
* Member experiences
* Profile discovery
* Premium feature presentation
* Important account moments
* Selected dark sections
* High-value calls to action

The private-club influence should communicate:

discretion, quality, exclusivity and sophistication

without making Ma3moni feel inaccessible.

⸻

3. THE OVERALL EMOTIONAL EXPERIENCE

When someone enters Ma3moni, the emotional progression should be:

Beautiful → Human → Trustworthy → Sophisticated → Intentional → Premium

The platform should communicate that Ma3moni is for people who are serious about meaningful relationships, without feeling overly formal or corporate.

Do not make it feel like:

* Tinder
* Bumble
* Hinge
* A generic matchmaking template
* A fintech dashboard
* A SaaS landing page
* A luxury hotel
* A fashion ecommerce website
* An AI-generated startup template

Ma3moni should establish its own visual identity.

⸻

4. REMOVE THE “AI-GENERATED WEBSITE” LOOK

This is one of the most important requirements.

Avoid visual patterns that immediately make a website feel AI-generated.

DO NOT OVERUSE:

* Glassmorphism
* Floating glass cards
* Excessive gradients
* Gradient text
* Giant oversized headings everywhere
* Excessive rounded rectangles
* Excessive pill-shaped buttons
* Floating decorative blobs
* Random abstract 3D objects
* Excessive glowing effects
* Neon accents
* Excessive shadows
* Perfectly symmetrical card grids
* Identical cards repeated across every section
* Generic stock illustrations
* Artificially perfect photography
* Excessive icons
* Decorative hearts
* Random geometric shapes

Do not use visual effects simply because they are fashionable.

Every visual element must have a purpose.

⸻

5. REDUCE “CARD EVERYTHING”

Do not place every piece of content inside a card.

Use a mixture of:

* Open layouts
* Full-width sections
* Editorial compositions
* Image-led sections
* Dividers
* Floating elements where appropriate
* Subtle containers
* Cards only when containment improves usability

Cards should be used intentionally.

The interface should breathe.

⸻

6. PHOTOGRAPHY DIRECTION

Photography is a major part of the Ma3moni visual identity.

Use cinematic, authentic African/Nigerian and globally relevant lifestyle photography.

Images should feel like real photography rather than generic stock imagery.

Prefer:

* Natural expressions
* Genuine interactions
* Real environments
* Lifestyle settings
* Elegant but believable clothing
* Natural lighting
* Soft daylight
* Golden-hour photography
* Evening environments
* Indoor lifestyle scenes
* Candid moments
* Environmental portraits
* Editorial portrait photography

Avoid:

* Everyone smiling directly into the camera
* Perfectly posed couples
* Corporate headshots
* Generic stock-photo compositions
* Unrealistically perfect models
* Repetitive poses
* Artificial-looking AI faces

People should look like real members of a sophisticated matchmaking community.

Use diversity naturally across:

* Gender
* Age within the platform’s target audience
* Skin tones
* Personal style
* Locations
* Personality
* Lifestyle

Do not stereotype Nigerian or African identity.

⸻

7. PROFILE PRESENTATION

Profiles are one of the most important parts of Ma3moni.

Make profile presentation feel editorial and human, not like ecommerce product cards.

Where the existing functionality allows it, prioritize:

* Strong portrait photography
* Natural image cropping
* Elegant typography
* Clear identity
* Location
* Profession/interests where already available
* Compatibility information where already available
* Short profile statements where already available

Avoid turning profiles into repetitive:

[PHOTO]
NAME
AGE
LOCATION
BUTTON

[PHOTO]
NAME
AGE
LOCATION
BUTTON

Instead, create visual rhythm while maintaining usability and consistency.

Profile images should feel like photographs of people, not product thumbnails.

⸻

8. TYPOGRAPHY

Create a sophisticated typography system.

Use:

Display typography

For major editorial moments and important headings.

It should feel elegant and distinctive without becoming theatrical.

Interface typography

For:

* Navigation
* Forms
* Buttons
* Filters
* Profile information
* Notifications
* Account settings
* System information

Prioritize:

* Readability
* Clear hierarchy
* Appropriate line height
* Controlled font weights
* Sophisticated spacing

Do not make every heading huge.

Do not use typography effects for decoration.

Use typography to create hierarchy naturally.

⸻

9. COLOR DIRECTION

Do not completely reinvent the existing Ma3moni brand unless necessary.

Refine the current visual palette into a sophisticated system.

The palette should feel:

* Warm
* Modern
* Elegant
* Mature
* Romantic without being childish
* Premium without being flashy

Use neutral surfaces extensively where appropriate.

Introduce darker tones selectively for:

* Premium areas
* Cinematic sections
* Footer
* Important brand moments
* Selected profile/discovery experiences

Avoid making the entire platform black.

IMPORTANT:

Do not automatically use:

* Gold everywhere
* Pink everywhere
* Purple gradients
* Red hearts
* Neon colors

Premium should come primarily from composition, photography, typography and execution, not expensive-looking colors.

⸻

10. LAYOUT SYSTEM

Create a consistent visual grid across the entire platform.

Maintain:

* Consistent page margins
* Consistent content widths
* Consistent spacing scale
* Consistent alignment
* Consistent typography hierarchy
* Consistent component dimensions
* Consistent responsive behavior

However, do not make every page visually identical.

Use controlled variation.

Some pages can be:

* Image-led
* Content-led
* Profile-led
* Form-led
* Editorial
* Dashboard-oriented

But they must all clearly belong to the same Ma3moni design system.

⸻

11. VISUAL RHYTHM

Avoid repetitive layouts.

Do not create:

Hero → 3 cards → 3 cards → 3 cards → CTA → footer

for every page.

Instead use visual rhythm through:

* Large and small imagery
* Different content widths
* Alternating compositions
* Open space
* Full-width photography
* Editorial sections
* Subtle dividers
* Controlled asymmetry

The variation must remain intentional and usable.

⸻

12. NAVIGATION

Do not redesign the navigation structure.

Preserve the existing navigation and its destinations.

Visually refine it using:

* Better spacing
* Better typography
* Refined active states
* Subtle borders or depth
* Better icon treatment
* Responsive behavior
* Clear hierarchy

Navigation should feel premium and effortless.

Avoid excessive floating navigation or experimental navigation patterns.

⸻

13. BUTTONS AND INTERACTIVE ELEMENTS

Buttons should feel tactile and premium.

Use:

* Clear hierarchy
* Strong primary action
* Subtle hover states
* Subtle pressed states
* Appropriate disabled states
* Consistent corner radius
* Consistent typography

Avoid:

* Giant buttons
* Excessive pill shapes
* Gradient buttons
* Glowing buttons
* Over-animation

Interaction should feel confident and understated.

⸻

14. FORMS AND ONBOARDING

Do not change the existing onboarding process.

Improve its visual experience.

Forms should feel:

* Calm
* Simple
* Personal
* Premium
* Easy to understand

Use appropriate photography or subtle visual storytelling where it enhances the existing flow.

Do not overwhelm users with decorative elements.

The experience should feel more like joining a sophisticated matchmaking community than filling out a generic web form.

⸻

15. DASHBOARD / MEMBER EXPERIENCE

The authenticated experience should remain consistent with the public-facing brand.

Do not suddenly turn the member area into a generic SaaS dashboard.

Maintain the Ma3moni visual identity through:

* Typography
* Photography
* Spacing
* Colors
* Component language
* Profile presentation
* Subtle editorial elements

However, usability takes priority inside functional areas.

The dashboard must remain practical and easy to navigate.

⸻

16. PREMIUM FEATURES

Where the existing product contains premium/subscription features, use the private-club influence more strongly.

Premium should feel:

* Exclusive
* Refined
* Valuable
* Discreet

Use:

* Richer imagery
* More sophisticated compositions
* Deeper tones
* Elegant typography
* Subtle visual distinction

Do not use obvious “luxury” clichés.

The user should feel:

“This feels special.”

not:

“This is covered in gold because it is premium.”

⸻

17. MATCHING AND CONNECTION MOMENTS

Where the existing functionality includes matches, interests or compatibility, make these moments feel emotionally meaningful.

Use subtle animation and visual emphasis.

Avoid cartoon-like effects.

Avoid excessive hearts and fireworks.

The interaction should feel:

personal, elegant and memorable.

⸻

18. MICRO-INTERACTIONS

Introduce subtle, purposeful motion.

Examples:

* Gentle image transitions
* Smooth page transitions
* Subtle hover movement
* Elegant profile-image reveals
* Refined button feedback
* Soft state transitions
* Subtle match/connection animation

Avoid:

* Bouncing
* Excessive scaling
* Floating everything
* Continuous animation
* Distracting parallax
* Decorative motion without purpose

Motion should communicate quality and responsiveness.

⸻

19. RESPONSIVE DESIGN

The visual upgrade must work across:

* Desktop
* Laptop
* Tablet
* Mobile

Do not simply shrink desktop layouts.

Preserve the visual character on smaller screens.

Photography, typography and spacing should adapt naturally.

Mobile should feel like a carefully designed Ma3moni experience, not a compressed desktop website.

⸻

20. DESIGN SYSTEM CONSISTENCY

Create a coherent visual system that can be applied across every existing Ma3moni screen.

Define and consistently reuse:

* Typography styles
* Colors
* Spacing
* Grid
* Buttons
* Form controls
* Inputs
* Cards
* Profile components
* Navigation
* Tabs
* Filters
* Modals
* Badges
* Notifications
* Empty states
* Loading states
* Error states
* Hover states
* Active states

If a component appears on multiple pages, it should look like the same component.

Do not redesign the same component differently on every page.

Consistency is more important than visual novelty.

⸻

21. IMPORTANT: DO NOT MAKE EVERY PAGE LOOK THE SAME

Consistency does NOT mean repetition.

The pages should share:

the same design language

without having:

the same layout.

For example:

Homepage → cinematic/editorial

Discovery → photography/profile-led

Profile → intimate/editorial

Messaging → clean and functional

Account → refined and practical

Premium → private-club sophistication

Forms → calm and elegant

Each should feel like Ma3moni.

⸻

22. REALISM OVER PERFECTION

The interface should contain the subtle imperfections that make human-designed products feel authentic.

Avoid making everything:

* Perfectly symmetrical
* Identically sized
* Identically spaced
* Identically rounded
* Perfectly centered

Use controlled asymmetry and natural visual rhythm.

However, never sacrifice usability for artistic composition.

⸻

23. CONTENT SHOULD FEEL HUMAN

Do not rewrite Ma3moni’s product messaging unless absolutely necessary for visual layout.

Preserve existing copy and meaning.

Do not fill the interface with generic AI copy such as:

“Find your perfect match today.”

“Where meaningful connections begin.”

“Discover your soulmate.”

If placeholder content is unavoidable, use realistic, natural language rather than cliché marketing copy.

25. BEFORE/AFTER THINKING

When modifying an existing component, ask:

Current:

Does this component look generic, overly card-based, overly symmetrical, too flat, too artificial or obviously AI-generated?

Upgrade:

Can the same component be made more:

* Natural?
* Editorial?
* Human?
* Premium?
* Cinematic?
* Usable?

without changing what it does?

If yes, make that visual improvement.

⸻

26. ABSOLUTE RESTRICTION ON FEATURE CHANGES

DO NOT:

* Add features
* Remove features
* Change workflows
* Change navigation destinations
* Change user permissions
* Change matchmaking logic
* Change subscription logic
* Change compatibility logic
* Change messaging functionality
* Change authentication
* Change forms’ business purpose
* Change the product’s information architecture

If something appears unclear, preserve the existing behavior rather than inventing a new one.

⸻

Yes.

Yes.

Yes.

Yes.

Yes.

Yes.

Yes.

Yes.

Yes.

Transform Ma3moni from an interface that may currently feel AI-generated, generic or template-driven into a product that feels like a carefully crafted premium matchmaking brand.

The visual reference point is:

Cinematic editorial design + contemporary romance + subtle private-club sophistication.

The design should feel:

Human-designed, emotionally intelligent, naturally beautiful and quietly premium.

Most importantly:

DO NOT REDESIGN MA3MONI. UPGRADE MA3MONI.

Preserve the product.

Preserve the functionality.

Preserve the process.

Preserve the user journey.

Change the visual experience.
